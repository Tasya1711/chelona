About us

Crafted with care, made to be one of a kind.

For the past five years, Chelóna has been creating handcrafted ceramics where no two pieces are ever the same. Every bowl, plate, mug and vase is individually designed, giving each creation its own character and story.

Alongside our original collections, we also bring our customers' ideas to life. Whether you have a sketch, an inspiration photo, or a completely unique concept, we transform your vision into a handcrafted ceramic piece made just for you.

Creativity doesn't stop at our workshop. Every week, we host pottery classes where anyone can experience the art of ceramics, learn traditional techniques, create their own unique piece and enjoy a welcoming, creative atmosphere.

Design in its purest form.

Every CHELÓNA piece is thrown, glazed and painted by hand — made to be the only one of its kind on your table.
